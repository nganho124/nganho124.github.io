I started in consulting because I wanted to solve real problems. Four years later, I'm still doing that — just with better tools and a deeper understanding of what the data is actually saying.

At CEL Consulting, I worked across supply chain networks in Australia, Europe, and Southeast Asia. The work was always the same at its core: take messy operational data, make sense of it, and turn it into a recommendation someone could actually act on. I've built dashboards, financial models, and simulation tools — and I've sat in the room when those outputs shaped million-dollar decisions.

Now I'm pursuing my MSc in Information Systems at FAU and working as a Student Assistant at HWR Berlin's Digital Supply Chain Lab, where I build simulation tools in Python and real-time dashboards in TypeScript. The domain has shifted — the drive to understand what the data is saying hasn't.

Most data students have projects. I have outcomes — 9.7M AUD saved through network consolidation, 3.7M EUR recovered from unprofitable SKUs, dashboards that replaced week-long reporting cycles. I write about this work, and what I'm learning, at todayonepercent.com.
