01. A single step

Even the longest journey starts with one step forward — and this is mine.

Ngan Ho · January 18, 2024 · 4 min read

A thousand miles journey starts with a single step.

— Laozi

This quote means, even the longest journey, all start with one step forward. I have a plan to start my blog again this year, after quite a long time from creating it without posting anything.

Why I want to write, and why now?

For me, noting down your experience, your lesson learned, and your mindset has a very special meaning. Looking back at my notes when I was 18 or 20, I realized that I was young, reckless, and confused — but also enthusiastic and energetic at the same time. Having these things recorded helps me to better understand my "current" version and helps shape the orientation for my future.

I was also impressed by this quote in Steve Jobs’s speech at Stanford’s university graduation ceremony in 2005. I once introduced this quote in the Power of Words section in my company weekly meeting, and recently the company partner also quoted it: “Connect the dots”. If you look back on anything that happened to you in the past, you can connect all of them and it will create the picture of yourself.

Another reason for writing is that in my work, there are a lot of interesting things that I want to note down and reflect on in a structured and organized way. The more I got older, the more I realized I couldn't remember all these details of my life — and some things are a pity to forget.

How this blog is organized

I will categorize my notes into different sections based on the name of the data types in Python — the most popular programming language in the data analytics field, the industry I'm currently working in.

String
Music, movies, art, and uncategorized thoughts 🎼
Numeric
Technical knowledge & sharing for Data & Consulting 🤖
Boolean
Perspectives, mindset, and how I think about right, wrong, or neither 🤓
Dictionary
Stories from working and study life 📘

I will try to bring both English, Vietnamese (updated: und Deutsch) versions for all of my blogs. Currently, I will prioritize Vietnamese — but in technical sharing, it would be better to write the terminologies in English and German, so hopefully your reading experience will not be impacted by the English words in the Vietnamese version.

So, that's it. The single step starts from here. January 18, 2024
